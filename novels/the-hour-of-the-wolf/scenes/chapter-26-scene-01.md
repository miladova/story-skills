# The records office and the two photographs

## Purpose

Warsaw in 1945: the office of the records of the reconstruction, a sister of nineteen who works there, and two photographs that are the whole of what survives of two families.

## Continuity Notes

Spring and summer 1945. The wedding photograph of 1911 and the photograph of 1928 with the works behind it; the note on the back of the second one is written decades after the events of this book and is not known to anybody in it.
