# The Marszałkowska, walked to the Świętokrzyska

## Purpose

The walk through the ruins in the heat: the Marszałkowska from the Plac Zbawiciela to the Świętokrzyska, the barricade stones being lifted, and the addresses of four people who are dead.

## Continuity Notes

First of August 1945, afternoon. The city has been rebuilt in the way that cities are rebuilt — with the old lines and the old names and almost nobody in the old rooms; the ledger of the war is a set of files in other people's hands.
