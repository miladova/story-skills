# The side chapel, at twenty to seven

## Purpose

The chapter's reaction beat: the morning after the ball, the chapel on the rue Saint-Honoré, and the decision she has already made without making it — the Polish word for the hour.

## Continuity Notes

Morning of the twenty-fifth of December 1943. She goes out into the rue Saint-Honoré at seven, touches the corner of an envelope in her pocket, and carries the name in her left hand and against the skin.
