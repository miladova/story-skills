# The flat on the Zielna, the fourth of July

## Purpose

The chapter's reaction beat: the flat with the piano and the curtain that does not move, the ultimatum restated in two sentences, and the week she has left to steal a fortress plan.

## Continuity Notes

Fourth of July 1944, the Zielna. Krupa offers her the alternative in writing; she does not answer him in the room, and the answer she has is the one the rest of the book is built on.
