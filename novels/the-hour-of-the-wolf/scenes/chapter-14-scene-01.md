# The patrol on the Hoża Street

## Purpose

The fourth of June, half past four in the afternoon: the patrol, the doorway, the papers, and the eleven boots that go past a shouting officer and a woman against a wall.

## Continuity Notes

The Hoża Street, Śródmieście; the corner of the Zielna. Nine minutes is the whole cause of it; the shouting is the whole of the cover; neither of them says a word for four minutes.
