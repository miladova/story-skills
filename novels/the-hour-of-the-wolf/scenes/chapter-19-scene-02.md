# The courtyard on Śliska Street, the armband

## Purpose

The grey coat comes off in a courtyard full of women, and a courier of seventeen ties an armband on an arm that has spent nineteen months in a German house.

## Continuity Notes

First of August 1944, the assembly point on Śliska Street, Śródmieście. Basia Marciniak's hands do the work a hundred times that day; the first rifle of the rising is heavier than it looks.
