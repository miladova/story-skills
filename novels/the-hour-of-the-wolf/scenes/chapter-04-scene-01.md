# The Christmas supper at the Majestic

## Purpose

The social set-piece of the Paris act: the ballroom, the wives, the champagne, the photograph taken for a newspaper, and the cost of being seen.

## Continuity Notes

Evening of the twenty-third of December 1943. Céline Marchand talks continuously; the car waits at the side door on the rue Dumont d'Urville with the breath of it showing.
