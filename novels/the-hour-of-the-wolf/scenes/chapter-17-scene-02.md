# The interview on the aleja Szucha

## Purpose

The twenty-sixth of July, nine in the morning: the summons, the corridor, and the interview with the man who has been reading the household of the Oberst for two months.

## Continuity Notes

Twenty-sixth of July 1944, Aleja Szucha 25. The pearls are on the table in the order in which they were bought; the stamp on her papers is Austrian and correct; she walks out at twenty to three with her face doing nothing at all.
