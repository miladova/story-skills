# Ten minutes to five, at the window

## Purpose

The first of August: the rehearsed hour, the two routes out of the house, the four routes out of the quarter, and the rattle of small arms heard from the north-west at four minutes to five.

## Continuity Notes

First of August 1944, ten minutes to five in the afternoon, the villa on Frascati Street. Rutz polishes the bonnet of the Mercedes; the officers in the drawing room go on talking; the first firing lasts nine seconds.
