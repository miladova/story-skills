# The blindfold, half past twelve at night

## Purpose

The interview that turns Phillip Brandt into a source: the papered windows, the card with the name on it, and the terms under which the section will have him.

## Continuity Notes

Night of the twenty-fifth of June 1944; the kitchen in the Żoliborz, used for this and nothing else. He asks to be sent where he can be useful; the section gives him a pseudonym and a handler, and the handler is the woman he came to save.
