# The junction under the Saxon Garden

## Purpose

The junction that goes wrong: nine of the nineteen out at the Saxon Garden, and the hour and forty minutes that she cannot afterwards remember except in pieces.

## Continuity Notes

Second of September 1944. Tadeusz Stankiewicz drowns there; Phillip Brandt comes out of the canal mouth at twenty minutes past eleven at night on the first; the crossing is the last thing the Old Town sends.
