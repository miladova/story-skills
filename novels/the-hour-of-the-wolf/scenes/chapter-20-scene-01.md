# The cellars, the fifth of August

## Purpose

Phillip's chapter: the fragments coming through the cellars, the first meeting with the woman who vouches for him, and the card with a name in the Major's handwriting.

## Continuity Notes

The first week of August 1944, Śródmieście. He is a German officer of the Abwehr in Polish civilian clothes with a bag of dressings, and the only reason he is not shot in a courtyard is a card.
