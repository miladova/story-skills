# The three reports of Basia

## Purpose

The nineteenth of August: the news arriving from three directions, the girl who was seventeen in May, and the work of writing a death down in the language of a file.

## Continuity Notes

Nineteenth of August 1944, half past four in the afternoon. Basia Marciniak is dead three ways in the telling and once in fact; the dispatches she was carrying went on.
