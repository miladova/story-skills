# The fitting on the rue de Miromesnil

## Purpose

The cover is maintained in the one place where it is real: the atelier, where an Austrian widow is fitted for a dress to wear in a German ballroom.

## Continuity Notes

Late November 1943, the first fitting of the winter. Odile Boin's rules for the workshop — the box under the cutting table, the pins in the mouth, the mouth kept shut.
