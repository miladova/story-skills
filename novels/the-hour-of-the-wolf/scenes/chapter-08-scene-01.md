# The house on the Frascati, walked room by room

## Purpose

The arrival in Warsaw: the villa requisitioned in the spring, the forty-minute walk through it with her coat still on, and the map of the new house being made in her head.

## Continuity Notes

Evening of the first of March 1944; the Villa on Frascati Street, in the district Poles may not walk in. The Colonel keeps the study and the safe; the rest of the house is hers to be seen in.
