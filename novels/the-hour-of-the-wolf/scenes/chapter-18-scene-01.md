# The safe, opened in front of her

## Purpose

The thirty-first of July at eleven: the first time in nineteen months that the safe is opened in front of her, for the fortress papers of the plan being revised weekly.

## Continuity Notes

Thirty-first of July 1944, the study of the villa. The plan is delivered in three parts; the papers are put back; the combination and the shape of the file are in her head by the time the door closes.
