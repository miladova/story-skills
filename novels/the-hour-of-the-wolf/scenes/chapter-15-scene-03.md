# The order: routine surveillance

## Purpose

The chapter's reaction beat and its hook: the second sheet in the correct form, the two watchers twice a week, and one additional name on the watch list in Germany's own German.

## Continuity Notes

Written on the Krucza Street on the night of the twenty-sixth of May 1944. The finding is that the surveillance is routine and shall not be theatrical; the consequence is a watch on Hoża Street that will be read, years later, by other people.
