# The ultimatum in the cellar

## Purpose

The twentieth of June in the cellar on the Śliska, with a map on the table: the order from the forest, the state of the garrison's paper, and the one person in Warsaw who has ever been inside that room.

## Continuity Notes

Twentieth of June 1944. The plan is a fortress plan and is being revised weekly, which means there are hours when it is on the desk and not in the safe; the alternative in the order is the identity of the person holding it.
