# The corner of the Bracka, half past five

## Purpose

The chapter's reaction beat: the violets bought and thrown away, the second-hand novel with four lines in the back cover, and the figure at the tram stop writing in a column.

## Continuity Notes

Nineteenth of March 1944, half past five in the afternoon, the Bracka and the Świętokrzyska. The man with the notebook is not writing down what he has seen; what he is keeping is a list of something else, and it will cost four people their lives.
