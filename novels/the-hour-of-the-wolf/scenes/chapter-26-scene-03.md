# The courtyard, four in the afternoon

## Purpose

The epilogue's last movement: the courtyard of a ruined building on the Mokotowska Street, the anchor still chalked on the wall, and the end of the hour between.

## Continuity Notes

First of August 1945, four in the afternoon, the Mokotowska Street. The ring is where it has been since 1940; the chalk anchor is where it was drawn on the thirty-first of July 1944; the name said out loud in that courtyard is the girl's name, and it is the last word of the book.
