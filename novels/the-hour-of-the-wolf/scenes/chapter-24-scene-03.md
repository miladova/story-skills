# The eleventh of September, on crutches

## Purpose

Phillip's work in the last weeks: the cellar of the printing house on the Świętokrzyska, the translating, and the three days that turn out to be nine.

## Continuity Notes

From the eleventh of September 1944. He is on crutches in a Polish coat sized for a larger man; the priest and an old woman hold the doorway; the office on Aleja Szucha is still reading its own files by candlelight.
