# Wierzbów, the last autumn

## Purpose

The book's first look back: the estate in its last autumn, the partridges, the boundary path, and the four things said by the ditch.

## Continuity Notes

Summer and autumn 1938, Wierzbów, thirty-one kilometres from Warsaw. Left undated on purpose so the flashback does not disturb the clock; the story date is in the prose. Phillip went back to Berlin; she had already decided on a man who would stay in Warsaw.
