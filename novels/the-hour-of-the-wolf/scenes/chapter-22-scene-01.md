# The descent into the canal

## Purpose

The crossing: the manhole, the brick arch three feet high, the chain bolted to the wall, and the guide who knows the route as a sequence of turns and loses it anyway.

## Continuity Notes

Night of the first and second of September 1944, the sewers between the Old Town and Śródmieście. The air is bad where the gas leaks; people drown in inches of water because the silt takes their feet; nobody who comes out of it is able to remember most of it.
