# The bed, and the courtyard grave

## Purpose

The second of October in the Mokotowska Street: the building that took two shells and a fire, the bed, the Horace, the blessing, and the burial in the courtyard under the chalk anchor.

## Continuity Notes

Second and third of October 1944. Jerzy Stankiewicz dies of his injuries on the third; four men dig the hole by the wall of the yard; the anchor is drawn over the place and the ring is on its cord round her neck.
