# The Wola, the sixth of August

## Purpose

The sixth of August in Wola: the open yards, the shoes standing in the road, the cellar, and the mother who says one word in German in the voice of a drawing room in 1936.

## Continuity Notes

Sixth of August 1944. Albrecht Brandt is dead in the courtyard of his own works; nineteen people are taken out of the district before dark; the route is the ruins of the ghetto and the gaps in the wall on the Bonifraterska.
