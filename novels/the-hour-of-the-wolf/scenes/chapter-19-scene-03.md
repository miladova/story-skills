# The study, until two in the morning

## Purpose

The chapter's reaction beat and its hook: the blackout curtain, the two fingers against the glass, and the question that ends the first act of the Warsaw book.

## Continuity Notes

Night of the first of August, until two in the morning. She has been out of the house and back into it; the Colonel stayed in the study; the question he asks from the window is the one the whole third act answers.
