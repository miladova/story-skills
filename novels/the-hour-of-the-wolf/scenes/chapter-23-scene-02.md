# Forty minutes at the table for twelve

## Purpose

The dinner: two places at one end of a table for twelve, four candles, a Burgundy of 1937, and the confession of a man of fifty-four who has decided he has nothing left to lose but her.

## Continuity Notes

Twenty-third of September 1944, the villa on Frascati Street. The substitution of the pearls is found out at that table; the discovery is what decides the hour the chapter is named for.
