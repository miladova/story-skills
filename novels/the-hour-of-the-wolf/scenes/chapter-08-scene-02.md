# Her own house, at twenty past eleven

## Purpose

The first walk out alone: the dress, the pretext, the town, and the palace on the Nowy Świat with a German office inside it.

## Continuity Notes

Second of March 1944, at twenty past eleven in the morning. The Stankiewicz palace is requisitioned; her father and sister are alive and unreachable; the walk is timed against the maid and the sentry.
