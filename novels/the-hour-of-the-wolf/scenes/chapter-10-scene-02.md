# The list, chewed in the dark

## Purpose

The chapter's reaction beat: standing against the garden wall with the list of the section's people in her mouth, eating paper mouthful by mouthful, and going up to bed in the house she is kept in.

## Continuity Notes

Night of the sixth of March 1944. The taste of paper stays with her; the Colonel sleeps on his back with his hands open; the sentry walks the gravel on his eleven minutes.
