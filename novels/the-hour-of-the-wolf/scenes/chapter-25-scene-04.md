# The clerk's table at Pruszków

## Purpose

The eighth of October at the transit camp: a hall that was a factory, a line that takes four hours and eleven minutes to move forty metres, and a clerk with a ledger, a rubber stamp, and a box of food cards.

## Continuity Notes

Eighth and twelfth of October 1944, Dulag 121, Pruszków. The transport for the west leaves at six; the section for the families of soldiers of the Home Army is in the second shed and she is not to go into it; the name that comes out of the camp is Maria Nowak.
