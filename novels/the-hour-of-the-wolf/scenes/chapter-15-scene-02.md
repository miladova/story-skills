# The interview with the orderly

## Purpose

The nineteenth of May: an hour and ten minutes with Rutz on the ground floor of the Szucha, and the day book of the household read back at him line by line.

## Continuity Notes

Nineteenth of May 1944. Rutz says nothing that is not true and nothing that is useful; the hours he does not write down are the only thing he keeps, and he keeps them until August.
