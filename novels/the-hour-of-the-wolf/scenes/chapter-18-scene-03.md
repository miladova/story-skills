# The plan delivered in three batches

## Purpose

The thirty-first of July, between eleven and four: the plan broken into three batches, carried across the centre of a city that is waiting, and put into the hands that will use it.

## Continuity Notes

The delivery is a place on the Śliska Street and a method; none of the three batches is ever in one room with another. This is the intelligence the rising's first week is fought on.
