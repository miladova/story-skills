# The flat on Mokotowska Street, the first of September

## Purpose

The second look back: the war arriving in the flat by radio at twenty minutes to six in the morning, the four of them in the hall, and the platform from which she is sent south.

## Continuity Notes

First to fourth of September 1939; undated on purpose, as the record above. The mother stays; the brother is in the east with the army; the sister is seventeen. This is the morning the country the family is named for stops existing.
