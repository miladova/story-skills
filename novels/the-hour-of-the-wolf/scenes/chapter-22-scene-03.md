# The order of the fourth of September

## Purpose

The chapter's reaction beat and its hook: the fourth-floor room with the whole roof of the city burning to the north, an order read twice, and what the second reading implies.

## Continuity Notes

Fourth of September 1944, eleven in the morning, the Świętokrzyska Street. The order puts the only two German translators of the rising in one room; there is a chart with eight names on it, a column headed language, and a question mark that is not in any Polish hand.
