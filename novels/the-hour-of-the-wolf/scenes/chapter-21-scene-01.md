# The cellar on Długa Street, the sixteenth of August

## Purpose

The intelligence room: the Długa Street cellar, the German prisoners, the translating, and the first interrogation conducted by a woman in Polish on behalf of a besieged staff.

## Continuity Notes

Sixteenth of August 1944, Długa Street, Śródmieście. The intelligence of a rising is taken out of ledgers, waterworks correspondence, and the mouths of men who are frightened of being useful.
