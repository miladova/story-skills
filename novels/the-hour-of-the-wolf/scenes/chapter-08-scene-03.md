# The coal queue in the snow

## Purpose

The chapter's reaction beat: the queue of thirty people in the snow, the depot, and the first rule of her Warsaw life — that the Colonel will not lose her in a street for the sake of a chemist's shop.

## Continuity Notes

Second of March 1944. Rutz waits with the car; the Colonel's sentences about a beautiful city that cannot be walked in are the terms of her imprisonment.
