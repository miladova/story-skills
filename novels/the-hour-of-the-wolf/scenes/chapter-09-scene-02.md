# Walking home in the snow

## Purpose

The meeting on the pavement in the snow: four seconds of looking, and one word said out loud in a city where a patrol can come round any corner.

## Continuity Notes

Sixteenth of March 1944. Hanna is seventeen and thin in a coat that was made for somebody else; the coat is turned the right way out again before Rutz and the car at the end of the street.
