# The flat in Wola

## Purpose

Phillip's chapter opens in the two rooms in Wola: the mother who speaks Polish when she is tired, the father who signed one paper to keep a workshop, and the piano that came out of the house on Hoża Street.

## Continuity Notes

March 1944, the third floor near the gasworks. The house on Hoża Street was requisitioned in May 1940; the photograph of 1928 and the piano are the two things that came to Wola out of it.
