# The ballroom of the Majestic

## Purpose

The Christmas ball: the dance watched by the Colonel, the exchange of four years in one measure, and the boy who has kept a name since 1938.

## Continuity Notes

Night of the twenty-fourth of December 1943, the ballroom of the Hôtel Majestic. The wrist, the orchard, and the four things said on the boundary path at Wierzbów — all of it rehearsed here and none of it said.
