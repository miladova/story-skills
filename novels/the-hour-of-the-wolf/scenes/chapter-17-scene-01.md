# The bag on the table

## Purpose

The twenty-fifth of July: the bag, the four documents, the offer of a man who has decided that the only clean thing left to do is dirty his hands on purpose.

## Continuity Notes

Twenty-fifth of July 1944, the Zielna. He has a card index problem of his own: every German in the city is going to have his name and his family in one before the summer is out, and the card that matters is the one about his mother.
