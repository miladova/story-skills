# The Mokotowska Street, twenty past nine

## Purpose

The last visit home before the rising: the courtyard, the chalk anchor drawn on the wall, the coat, and the ninety minutes the family is allowed to have.

## Continuity Notes

Thirty-first of July 1944, twenty past nine in the morning, Mokotowska Street. The anchor is drawn for a reason the book explains at the end; the coat that will carry the papers out of the city is in this flat.
