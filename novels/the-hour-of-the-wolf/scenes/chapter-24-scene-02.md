# The cellar on the Madalińskiego Street

## Purpose

The end of the hunt: the brick vault, the well, the doorway, and the shot that settles the last of the German officers of the book.

## Continuity Notes

Late September 1944, the Madalińskiego Street, Mokotów. Kalbach is shot in a doorway with his notebook in his pocket; the four sources he had assembled die with him; the ring goes back on its cord.
