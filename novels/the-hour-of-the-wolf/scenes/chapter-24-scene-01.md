# The search of the flat on the Mokotowska Street

## Purpose

The search: the flat, the men, the inventory, and the moment the hunter who has been reading a household for two months stands in the middle of it with his hat in his hand.

## Continuity Notes

Ninth of September 1944, the flat on Mokotowska Street, Śródmieście. Kalbach walks the rooms himself; the inventory he takes away is the list that decides the last three weeks of the book.
