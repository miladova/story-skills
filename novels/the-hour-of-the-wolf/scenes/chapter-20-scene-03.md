# The eighth of August, sent for by the staff

## Purpose

The chapter's reaction beat: the Błyskawica boy with his helmet in his hand, the flat ordinary delivery of the largest news of that week, and the room that has to decide what to do with a German in it.

## Continuity Notes

Eighth of August 1944, six in the evening, Śródmieście. Basia Marciniak was killed on the Marszałkowska barricade; the dispatches went on; the man on the stairs picked them up.
