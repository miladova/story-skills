# The second of October, the guns stopped

## Purpose

The chapter's reaction beat and its hook: eight in the evening on the second of October, the guns stopping by an agreement signed in a tent in the forest, and a courtyard on the Wilcza where a staff with eleven hours left burns its files.

## Continuity Notes

Second of October 1944. The order goes out in the language of a capitulation: the soldiers of the Home Army are to present themselves for captivity, and the civilians are to leave the city. What is left for the two of them is four streets and the last of the paper.
