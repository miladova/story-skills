# The garden, the open gate, the car

## Purpose

The chapter's reaction beat and its hook: four metres from the wall of the neighbouring house, the light of a fire coming up over the roofs, and the three things it shows her.

## Continuity Notes

Twenty-fourth of September 1944, before dawn. The gate is open, the sentry box is empty, and the man in the car is the hunter with nine pages of a chart on his knee — the moment that opens the last act.
