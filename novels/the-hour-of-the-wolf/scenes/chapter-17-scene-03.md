# The last night of July, with schnapps

## Purpose

The chapter's reaction beat and its hook: the tunic unbuttoned, the schnapps, the hand that finds her wrist in the dark not as an owner but as a man who has found the only person he can talk to.

## Continuity Notes

Night of the thirty-first of July 1944, the Villa on Frascati Street. Twenty-four hours before W-hour. She sees the whole of it, in the dark, and cannot unsee it — and the book says so plainly.
