# Rutz's day book

## Clue

The Colonel's orderly keeps a small notebook in his tunic: the car's mileage, the hours of the sentries, what was drunk at dinner. In chapter 4 Krystyna sees him write *Fräulein Marion* and the time she came in, and then see her looking, and close the notebook.

## Planting Plan

Played as a small domestic humiliation: the mistress of the house being counted in a servant's book. Her terror should be invisible to him.

## Payoff Plan

Chapter 19, at W-hour, she walks out of the gate past Rutz with the papers in her coat, and he salutes her, and when the Colonel asks which way the lady went he says west, and she went east. He is shot for it in a courtyard in Wola before the week is out, and nobody in the book learns except the reader.

## Tracking Notes

The book appears three times and is never read by anyone but Rutz.
