# The blotter

## Clue

The Colonel's desk in Paris has a leather blotter, and because he is a meticulous man he does not throw away the used sheets; he stacks them, face down, under the present one. A mirror-read of a letter's reverse is how Krystyna learns that the SD is planting a document in the Majestic to catch the leak.

## Planting Plan

Chapter 1, as furniture: she is sitting at the desk pretending to write a note to her dressmaker.

## Payoff Plan

Chapter 7: she recognises the trap from the blank paper, not from the plot - the paper is new, the ink is fresh, and the Colonel's own office has never once had a clean sheet of that weight in it. She survives it by doing the cruellest thing in Part One; the blotter is the clue that saves her and the reader should connect the two without help.

## Tracking Notes

The blotter travels to Warsaw with the desk and appears in chapter 23 in the villa on Frascati Street, with a different stack of sheets in it.
