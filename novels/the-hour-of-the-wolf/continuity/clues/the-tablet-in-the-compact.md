# The tablet in the compact

## Clue

A silver compact with a false floor and one tablet in it, given to her by Wiktor with a rule: *You will not be taken alive and talking. This is not courage. This is tradecraft.* It comes out of her handbag four times in the book and is used zero times, until a windowsill on the morning of the capitulation.

## Planting Plan

Chapter 2, coldly, in Wiktor's voice, with no sentiment: the object should frighten the reader on her behalf.

## Payoff Plan

Chapter 23: von Hagen finds it, opens it, finds powder, keeps it in his desk drawer, and she has to leave the house without it - so that the instrument of her escape is unavailable at the exact moment she needs it, which is the definition of this book's kind of luck. Chapter 25: on the windowsill of the ruined flat, on the morning the column forms, she tips the tablet into the rubble and does not replace it, and the choice is the last one she makes in the war.

## Tracking Notes

Never let the tablet be described as a symbol. It is an exit.
