# The signet ring

## Clue

A silver signet on a cord inside her blouse, never seen by any German: a greyhound's head above three willow leaves, the crest of the Stankiewicz family. It is her father's, given to her in the hall while the German removals men carried out the sofa.

## Planting Plan

Chapter 2, in the atelier, when she undresses for a fitting and Odile asks what it is. She says it is her mother's.

## Payoff Plan

Chapter 24: it is in Kalbach's evidence envelope, taken from a glove in the flat on Mokotowska Street during a search in August, and after Kalbach dies Phillip puts it back in her hand. The ring's journey is the plot: identity as the thing that can kill you, restored by the person who loves you.

## Tracking Notes

Track the ring's custodian after chapter 22 or the payoff will be a cheat.
