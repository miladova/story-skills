# The chalk anchor

## Clue

The Home Army's kotwica - an anchor made of a P and a W - appears on walls all over Warsaw, chalked by teenagers in the night. Hanna draws one on the wall of the courtyard at Mokotowska Street every time her sister visits, so that if the flat is raided the mark will be found and read by people who will know what it means.

## Planting Plan

Chapter 10, played as a child's bravado in a doorway, with Tadeusz furious about it.

## Payoff Plan

The epilogue: on a heap of rubble that used to be a street corner, somebody has chalked the anchor again, in 1945, and it is the second thing Krystyna sees on her first morning back in Warsaw. The first is the man.

## Tracking Notes

The mark is a marker of survival, never of hope in general.
