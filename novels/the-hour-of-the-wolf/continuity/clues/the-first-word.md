# The word that came first

## Clue

At tea in the Majestic, Marion Herschlag asks the orderly for a *bułka* - the Polish word for a roll - and covers it instantly with a story about a Galician nanny. Everyone laughs. Kalbach does not laugh; he writes nothing down and remembers it.

## Planting Plan

Chapter 3, played as comedy in a room full of Germans: the reader should enjoy the escape and only later understand that it was the first entry in the file.

## Payoff Plan

In chapter 15 Kalbach's chart has a column headed *language*: a witness's memory of a word, a Viennese accent with a Warsaw vowel in it, and a dressmaker's invoice in French paid in occupation marks. The word is what puts her on the chart; it is not what proves anything, and that is the point about his method.

## Tracking Notes

Do not let any other character notice the slip. It belongs to Kalbach alone.
