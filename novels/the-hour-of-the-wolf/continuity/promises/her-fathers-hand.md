# Her father's hand

## Setup

Chapter 19: at dawn on the last day of peace, she comes to the flat on Mokotowska Street and her father stands up from the table and goes into the other room. What is on the page is his back. What is planted is what his hand does on the way: he steadies himself on the doorframe, which she has not seen him do in her life.

## Payoff

Chapter 25, on the morning of the capitulation: the same doorframe, the same hand, and this time he reaches for her. He asks her to read him a line of Horace, and she cannot remember it, and Hanna says it from the corridor, and he dies in the interval between two lines of the Odes.

## Tracking Notes

The hand, not the face. He never turns around in between.
