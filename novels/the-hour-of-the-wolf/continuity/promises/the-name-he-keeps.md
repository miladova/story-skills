# The name he keeps

## Setup

Chapter 5, in a corridor of the Hôtel Majestic: Phillip Brandt says *Krystyna* out loud to a woman the German staff calls Marion Herschlag, and spends the rest of the war not saying it again in any place where it can be heard.

## Payoff

The epilogue. The last line of the book is her name in his mouth in a ruined street, without a mask over it and without a German in earshot, which is the whole plot of the novel in five words.

## Tracking Notes

Between the planting and the payoff he says it twice more in the text, both times whispered, and the reader should count them.
