# Wiktor's third rule

## Setup

Chapter 2: *You will not be taken alive and talking.* Wiktor's rules are given to her as a catechism in the atelier, with the compact in her hand, and she repeats them back to him, because that is the training.

## Payoff

Chapter 25, at the fence, when the column is forming and she has been awake for two days: for the first time since Paris she thinks the whole rule through, decides it is not hers any more, and walks east into the queue at Dulag 121 with a live body and a false name. The rule is what she obeys for four years and what she finally, deliberately disobeys; the epilogue's survival is the consequence.

## Tracking Notes

The rule is quoted in full exactly twice: at the planting and at the payoff.
