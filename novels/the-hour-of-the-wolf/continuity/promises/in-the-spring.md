# In the spring

## Setup

Chapter 25, on the road out of the ruins, at the fence: he says it first, as a fact, because Phillip states things - *I will find you in the spring* - and she says it back because she cannot say goodbye, which is the closest either of them has come to a declaration in twenty-six chapters.

## Payoff

The epilogue: 1 August 1945. Neither of them says it out loud when they meet. The reader is the one who remembers that it was said, and the last page answers it with something that is neither a wedding nor a grave.

## Tracking Notes

Do not repeat the phrase between its planting and its payoff, not once, in anyone's mouth or head.
