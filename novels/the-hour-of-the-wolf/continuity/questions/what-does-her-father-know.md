# What does her father know?

Introduced by the AK's ruling that the family must be told she is dead. Answered twice: once when he reads the truth off a German notice in chapter 19, and once in chapter 25, when he says her name and asks her for a line of Horace, which is the only blessing this man is capable of giving.
