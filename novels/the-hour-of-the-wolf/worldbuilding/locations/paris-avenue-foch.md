# The Sicherheitspolizei office, avenue Foch

A stone building of 1900 on the avenue Foch, close to the Bois: the Paris seat of the Sicherheitspolizei and the SD, with a courtyard full of cars, a canteen below stairs, and a section on the second floor where a man called Kalbach keeps his charts.


The building in which the case called Fräulein is opened on the fourteenth of January 1944, and the office to which the file goes in August 1944 when its officer is transferred east to Warsaw.

It is the last address in Paris that Krystyna ever has to know: the number she dials at ten past four in the afternoon of the seventh of January to place a Frenchwoman in a police car, and the building she never once enters.
