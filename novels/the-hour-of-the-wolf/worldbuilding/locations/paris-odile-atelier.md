# Boin, Robes et Manteaux

A dressmaker's workroom behind a green shopfront: three windows on a courtyard, bolts of cloth stacked to the ceiling, a long table, and drawers whose lining hides a press that makes ration cards.


The sixth point of the network and the safest-looking place she goes. Odile Boin fits Marion Herschlag for everything she wears in this book; the same room, two hours later, prints the papers that let a Polish boy from the 14th arrondissement get a job in Normandy out of the German labour round-ups.

## Smells And Sounds

Chalk, sizing, scorched silk, the iron hissing; the courtyard cats; the bell over the door that rings in a key it should not; the tram on the boulevard when the wind is from the north.
