# The Old Town

The oldest quarter of Warsaw: the Długa and the Miodowa, the Rynek and the Barbican, the cathedral and the houses of the burghers, held by the Polish Home Army from the first of August 1944 and reduced house by house through September by artillery, flame and assault groups.


The district that is invested and taken apart in the last days of August and the first days of September 1944: thirty thousand people, of whom eight hundred are wounded, in a quarter of a mile of streets under artillery fire that is directed from four hundred metres away.

This is where Krystyna spends the rising reading German: the intelligence section in the back rooms on the Długa Street, the captain with the wounded hand, the priest who carries the satchel, the interrogation of a boy of twenty-two from a signals company, and the burning of the section's archive in the courtyard on the first of September before the crossing into the sewers.
