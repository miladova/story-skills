# The Stankiewicz palace, Nowy Świat

A neoclassical town palace with a courtyard and a Doric gate, the family's arms over the pediment, and, in 1944, a painted German eagle on a sign reading MILITÄR-KRAFTFAHRPARK: a motor pool with lorries in the courtyard where the carriages used to stand.


Requisitioned in 1940 for a German motorised transport office and turned over, as the family was told with bureaucratic courtesy, "for the duration of the present emergency". In the courtyard where Krystyna's mother used to hold a Christmas tree for the servants' children, there are now two lorries with their bonnets up and a mechanic's lamp burning all night.

She passes it eleven times in this book. It is never mentioned twice in the same way.
