# The Stankiewicz flat, Mokotowska Street

A first-floor flat of four rooms, two of them let to a schoolmaster's widow, one kitchen with a stove that draws badly, a corridor with a brass umbrella stand that is the only thing left of the old house's furniture, and a pianino nobody has tuned since 1939.


What is left of a family with a palace, a portrait gallery and an estate they sold in 1932 to pay for a sugar-beet venture. The flat should feel exactly like that: good things in a small space, a maid's bell with no maid, the smell of cabbage and floor wax, a father's books and nowhere to put all of them so they stand in rows on the floor along the wall.

The window over the courtyard is the one place in Warsaw Krystyna is not permitted to stand in.
