# Pawiak Prison

On Dzielna Street behind the ruins of the ghetto: red brick, a heavy gate, one wing for men and one for women, and a window in the men's block that faces the courtyard where the executions happen.


Where the city's arrested go. The place where a courier's name begins to travel: Pawiak, Szucha, the ghetto ruins, a notice posted on a wall with the names of a hundred others, and a family queueing at a window on Dzielna Street with a parcel that will be returned.

Every character in Part Two behaves differently when a friend is sent to Pawiak, and the difference is the illustration of the city's arithmetic: the parcel queues, the bribes, the letters smuggled in boot heels, the mass of small industry that grew around the apparatus of killing.
