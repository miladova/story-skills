# Aleja Szucha 25

The Sicherheitspolizei's Warsaw seat: a great building on a square of the same name, a courtyard with cars under the lindens, cells in the basement, and the only building in the city that Poles will not walk past on the near pavement.


The torture house, in the city's own words. Solitary cells in the basement; interrogations that begin politely and end in the disinfection room; a courtyard in which more than one person has been shot and left until morning to prove a point.

The building's horror is administrative. There are typists. There is a canteen. Officers complain about the coffee, and Kalbach's office has a window on the lindens, and everything that happens three floors below is written down in three copies.
