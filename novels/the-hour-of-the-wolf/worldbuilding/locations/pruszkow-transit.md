# Dulag 121, Pruszków

A former rolling-stock works by the railway line west of Warsaw: open ground, wire, military trains standing on the sidings, the smoke of a city still burning behind the fence, and a hundred thousand people at a time who are being registered, robbed, sorted and sent on.


Where Warsaw's expelled population is taken in October 1944. Sixty-odd thousand in the first days, and the majority moved on to labour or to camps. Everything anyone has seen about it in Part Three is a very small part of it, and the book never takes the wide view here: this is a place where a family loses each other in a queue, and where the last of the Stankiewiczs has to decide, in a line of cattle trucks, what she is prepared to become in order to survive it.
