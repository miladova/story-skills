# The canals

Nine hundred and some metres of brick sewer at a height of between three and five feet, running with water and silt at the bottom, a chain bolted to the wall where the current is dangerous, and total darkness except for a torch held between the teeth.


Rising ground in September 1944: the only way out of the Old Town, and later out of Mokotów, for soldiers, for couriers with dispatches, and for the wounded on stretchers held above the water by four people who cannot see their own feet.

The book's rules for the canals are documentary: the guides know the route as a sequence of junctions and turns, and lose it anyway; people drown in four inches of water because the silt takes their feet; the air is bad in the low passages, especially where the gas leaks; and someone always cracks, and the rest have to carry them along.
