# The szmalcownicy

Blackmailers, on both sides of the wall's memory: men and women who make their living from the fear of people in hiding, and later from anyone carrying a face or a name that could be sold to the police. They are not organised, which is worse; they are a market, and the market sets prices.

The book's use of them is precise and local: one of them recognises a face that Germans would pay for, and the recognition is ordinary, sordid and lethal, and settling it costs a life and a piece of somebody's soul. There is nothing heroic in the transaction on either side, and it produces no witnesses.
