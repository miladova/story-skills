# The Sicherheitspolizei and SD

The political police: the card indexes, the interrogations, the cells, the executions, and the two buildings in the book that no character walks past without a physical reaction. It is the only institution in the novel that cannot be managed, only avoided, and the avoidance is the plot.

Its officers are not the story's monsters; they are its most competent men. Kalbach's section is quiet, tidy, well-documented, and it is going to find her, and the reader knows it from the first page of Part Two.

Faction note for drafting: never let a character out-argue the SD, and never let an escape be clean. If the SD loses a step in this book, it is because somebody paid something: a name, a body, an alibi, a life.
