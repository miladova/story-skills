# The silver compact

A woman's compact, engine-turned silver, with a mirror and a false floor of a quarter of an inch under the powder tray. Wiktor gave it to her in 1942 with a tablet in it and a sentence: *You will not be taken alive and talking. This is not courage. This is tradecraft.*

The tablet in it is the only thing in the book that Krystyna is frightened of. It is opened four times: in the alley behind the Majestic (she does not take it); in the flat on Frascati Street when the Colonel finds it on her dressing table and asks what women keep in these (she says powder, and he believes her, and keeps it in his own drawer, which she does not discover until the chapter in which he dies); in Kalbach's hand at aleja Szucha; and on the morning of the capitulation, on a windowsill, where it is finally used for something else.
