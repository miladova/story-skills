# The pearls

A single strand of graduated pearls, bought in Paris in 1941 from a jeweller on the rue de la Paix who was selling the stock of people who had left, and fastened round her neck by von Hagen on the night he decided she was his.

She wears them for him and hates them; she pawns them in Warsaw in chapter 13 to pay for a dead courier's family to leave the city, replacing them with paste; and the substitution is discovered in chapter 23 by a man who knows exactly how much money he spent, at the worst possible moment, which is what the object is for.
