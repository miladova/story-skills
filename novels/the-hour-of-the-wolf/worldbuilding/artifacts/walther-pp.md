# The Colonel's Walther

A PP in a shoulder holster he never wears, kept in the top left drawer of the study desk at Frascati Street with a spare magazine and a cleaning cloth folded to a square.

The book's rules for the gun: she has known where it is since her second week in the house; she has never once touched it before chapter 23; and when it is finally fired the room is small, the sound is not thrilling, and the aftermath is not a scene of triumph but twenty minutes of very practical terror with a body on the carpet, a man's blood on a woman's forearms, and the urgent need to be extremely quiet at four in the morning in a city under siege.
