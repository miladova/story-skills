# Kurt Lindemann (Hauptmann)

## Appearance

Thirty-three, handsome in an over-designed way, fond of his uniform's cut, curls his hair with a hot finger. Good teeth, good French, a laugh he uses the way other men use a bayonet.

## Personality & Traits

An opportunist of the sort every intelligence service breeds in wartime. He is a colleague, not an enemy; there is no enmity in him at all, which is what makes him dangerous: he will report Phillip to the SD without malice if the arithmetic encourages it, and he will drink with him on the evening of the same day.

He is also, to the general discomfort of the book, good company and often right. The reader should enjoy his scenes and then feel bad about it.

## Voice & Speech Patterns

Confiding, ironic, conspiratorial — *between us*, *naturally* — with a taste for French phrases and a radio-voice cadence. He talks about the war as a business cycle and about Warsaw as a posting.

> "Between us, Phillip, a man with Polish like yours and no wife is either an idealist or an idiot, and I have never known an idealist to survive a winter."
