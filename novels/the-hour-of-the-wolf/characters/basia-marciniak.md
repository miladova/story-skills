# Basia Marciniak

## Appearance

Seventeen, small, fast, with a boy's cap shoved in her coat and terrible haircuts she gives herself with nail scissors. Braces that she never had so they are crooked anyway. Runs with her elbows out and her chin down and her cape flying, and the whole district knows her whistle.

## Personality & Traits

The Rising's youth, done without sentimentality: brave, silly, obstinate, quick with a joke, ruthless about German prisoners and tender with children. She treats Krystyna with the awe due a legend and the familiarity due a neighbour, and she is the only character who asks her, flat out, what it is like to sleep in a German's bed — in a cellar, in the dark, while the bombs fall three streets over.

Her death is one of the book's facts and not one of its symbols: she was carrying dispatches, she was seen, she was killed. The dispatches arrive.

## Voice & Speech Patterns

Young Warsaw: syllables clipped, everything in the present tense, says *so* and *you know* and *mhm* between clauses, calls her *Madame Marion* as a joke, then *Krysia* when the bombs are close.
