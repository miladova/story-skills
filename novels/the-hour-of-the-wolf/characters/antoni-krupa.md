# Antoni Krupa (Major "Sęk")

## Appearance

Forty-seven, a lawyer in his other life, going grey and going fat on ersatz coffee. Spectacles, a shabby suit, a way of standing that is half at attention. He takes his spectacles off to think; in the war's fourth year the bridge of his nose has two permanent dents from it.

## Personality & Traits

A man doing an impossible job with insufficient paper. He runs counter-intelligence in a city where the Germans read the walls and the walls read back. He is just, procedural, and capable of enormous cruelty with a clear conscience, because he has decided that the group is the only morality available and that any individual can be spent for it, including himself, including her.

He has one soft spot, and it is competence: he does not care what a person is, only what they deliver next. This is why he takes Krystyna's arrangement and, later, her German.

## Voice & Speech Patterns

Legal and exact; he states the terms and then the consequence. He uses the conditional when he is forbidding something: *That would be very bad for us, and for her mother's pension.* He never raises his voice but he has a way of saying the word *understand* that ends arguments.

> "You have brought me gold, and it was carried through a German's house on the hips of a Polish girl. I am not a priest. Bring more."
