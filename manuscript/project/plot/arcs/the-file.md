# The File

## Setup

A Party official is ordered to stop a priest from becoming a bishop, using a file that contains both his 1943 confession and her 1945 signature.

## Rising Action

1. Halina delivers the ultimatum and finds herself confessing to the man she was sent to break.
2. The priest refuses the Commission and preaches the truth about the dead worker.
3. The priest confesses the file's contents from his own pulpit before the state can publish it.

## Climax

On the fourth Sunday of Advent the priest tells the whole parish what he did in 1943, taking the weapon out of the state's hands; the state refuses his appointment anyway, and appoints its own man.

## Resolution

The official confesses to the widow and is expelled; the priest goes to a country parish; nobody is punished for Berezowski except the one who chose to be. The file stays closed because it has stopped being a weapon and become a record.
