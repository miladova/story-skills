# Continuity State

## Current Story State

The book ends in September 1959. Halina Sowa has been expelled from the Party, has given up the flat on Marszałkowska for a room in Praga, and works four mornings a week as a bookkeeper at a clinic on Radzymińska Street while training as a village teacher; she plans to take up the first class at the school in Brzóza in September 1960. Janusz Wilga was refused the auxiliary bishopric in December 1958, confessed his 1943 betrayal from his own pulpit on the fourth Sunday of Advent, and was transferred to a parish in Wyszków in February 1959. His confession and the state's refusal are public; the file stayed closed. Rydel kept Halina's written confession in his drawer rather than sending it up. Berezowski's widow knows the truth. Father Piotr is in a country parish.
