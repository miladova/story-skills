# Agnieszka Rydel

## Appearance

Twelve, a red hat, a ribbon in her hair that does not match her dress.

## Personality & Traits

Direct, cheerful, unafraid to ask the question adults cannot answer.

## Backstory

Rydel's daughter; her mother is dead; her aunt takes her to Mass on Miodowa Street.

## Motivations & Goals

To know whether the things she is told are true.

## Voice & Speech Patterns

- "Do you believe in the baby Jesus?"

## Character Arc

- **Starting state:** A child in the back of a black car.
- **Key turning points:** Asking her father the question he cannot answer.
- **Ending state:** The next generation, seen through a car window.
