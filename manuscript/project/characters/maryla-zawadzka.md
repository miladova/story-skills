# Maryla Zawadzka

## Appearance

Short, round-faced, gray-haired, perpetually in a red coat and knitted hat; flour on her hands at Christmas.

## Personality & Traits

Warm, blunt, competent; a doctor at a Mokotów clinic who has spent her life taking care of other people and has no patience with self-dramatisation. The only person who telephones Halina at home.

## Backstory

From Brzóza; she and Halina grew up together. She told Halina about Rutka. She has invited Halina to Christmas Eve every year for eleven years. Married to Kazimierz, an engineer; daughter Ewa in medical school, son Marek in the army.

## Motivations & Goals

To keep the people she loves in one room, fed.

## Voice & Speech Patterns

Domestic, commanding, unsentimental.

- "You are early. You are never early."
- "Sit up straight and do not put your elbow in the compote."
- "You are not a bad person. You are a person who has been asked to do bad things."

## Character Arc

- **Starting state:** Halina's one ordinary friendship.
- **Key turning points:** Inviting Janusz to Wigilia; hosting the evening that puts them in one room.
- **Ending state:** The keeper of the ordinary; the one who asks the question nobody else will.
