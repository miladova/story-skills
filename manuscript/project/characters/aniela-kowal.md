# Aniela Kowal

## Appearance

A peasant woman with strong arms and a worn face; in Halina's memory she is always in the kitchen.

## Personality & Traits

Devout, stubborn, quietly brave. Hid Rutka Kreiner in the attic for two weeks, was beaten by the Germans for it, and never spoke of it.

## Backstory

Wife of Stanisław Kowal, who was taken for forced labour 1939–41. Mother of Halina and Tadeusz. Wrote to Janusz in 1957 asking him to pray for her daughter. Died in November 1958.
