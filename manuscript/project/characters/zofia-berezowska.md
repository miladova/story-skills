# Zofia Berezowska

## Appearance

Fifty-two, small and upright, gray hair pinned back, an apron; a face that has been kept careful for thirteen years.

## Personality & Traits

Composed, dignified, and hard as a stone underneath. She has told her children their father died of a heart attack for thirteen years and has never believed it.

## Backstory

Widow of Wiktor Berezowski, the AK commander shot near Lublin in 1945 after Halina's report. Lives in Otwock near her son; her daughter is at a technical school in Warsaw.

## Motivations & Goals

To know what was done to her husband, and to have it said aloud.

## Voice & Speech Patterns

Formal, controlled, and devastating in short sentences.

- "Come in before the neighbours see you crying."
- "Now you come and you tell me that a woman signed the paper."
- "In this house nobody gets up from a table on Christmas Eve."

## Character Arc

- **Starting state:** The keeper of a lie.
- **Key turning points:** Halina's confession in her kitchen.
- **Ending state:** A woman who knows.
