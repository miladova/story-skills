# Father Piotr Nowicki

## Appearance

Twenty-six, round-faced, small mustache, nervous energy; a former electrician.

## Personality & Traits

Earnest, anxious, better with the young than with secrets. He is not a coward and not a hero; he is a young man who has been frightened by professionals and has discovered what he is made of.

## Backstory

Two years at the Transfiguration. Pressured by the Security Service about his widowed mother's pension and his brother's job, he informed on Janusz for a year. Arrested in December, interrogated by Major Zawistowski, released through Halina's intervention, and confesses to Janusz.

## Motivations & Goals

To keep his mother safe and to be a good priest; the two become incompatible.

## Voice & Speech Patterns

Rapid, self-accusing, apologetic.

- "I gave him the names. He wrote them down."
- "Now you will send me away."
- "I am a coward."

## Character Arc

- **Starting state:** A cheerful young curate with a secret.
- **Key turning points:** The interrogation; the confession to Janusz; being forgiven.
- **Ending state:** Transferred to a country parish, forgiven, and sent away for his own good.
