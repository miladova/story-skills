# Pani Genowefa

## Appearance

Small, stout, white-haired, red-faced. Arms folded.

## Personality & Traits

Formidable, warm, allergic to nonsense, an unsentimental judge of character. Treats the priests as her children and the parish as her property.

## Backstory

Widow of the Warsaw Uprising; no children; twenty years keeping house for the parish.

## Motivations & Goals

That the boiler works, the priests eat, and the Church survives the state.

## Voice & Speech Patterns

Flat, practical, devastating.

- "You are going to see the cardinal. Wear the good cassock."
- "She has a cold heart." / "No. She has a broken one."
- "The kettle is broke. It has been broke since Thursday."

## Character Arc

- **Starting state:** The keeper of the rectory.
- **Key turning points:** Seating the security men in the fourth row deliberately; watching the youth club furniture carried away.
- **Ending state:** Writing to Wyszków once a week.
