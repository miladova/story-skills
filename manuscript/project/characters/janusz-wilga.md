# Janusz Wilga

## Appearance

Thirty-six. Lean, dark-haired, with a scar above the left eyebrow and the broad hands of a blacksmith's son. Wears his cassock to a shine at the elbows and never turns up his collar. His face is a priest's face in the old style: watchful, unguarded in repose.

## Personality & Traits

Quiet, stubborn, physically brave and morally scrupulous to the point of self-punishment. A superb preacher in the plain style, better with workers and children than with chanceries. He is not a mystic; he is a man who has been broken once and has decided that the only honest way to live afterwards is to keep telling the truth. Hates the word "martyr."

## Backstory

Born 22 November 1922 in Brzóza, son of Franciszek the blacksmith and Jadwiga. Altar boy, then AK courier under Berezowski (pseudonym Sosna, "pine"). Arrested by the Gestapo in Lublin on 12 August 1943, tortured for three days, broke when shown his mother's photograph, and gave up the hideout. Three men were killed on 14 August, including Tadek Kowal. Released 20 August. Entered the underground seminary, ordained 1948, curate and then rector at the Transfiguration on Brzeska Street. In 1958 the Primate proposes him as auxiliary bishop of Warsaw.

## Motivations & Goals

Wants to be a good parish priest; needs to be able to stand in his own pulpit without lying. The bishopric is a cross he does not seek and a temptation (of safety, of influence) that he must refuse on his own terms.

## Voice & Speech Patterns

Plain, concrete, scriptural without unction; he ends arguments with a fact rather than a flourish.

- "A man who is not afraid is a fool. Fear is not a sin."
- "I am not negotiating. I am going."
- "It is not a sacrifice. It is a thing that happened to me, in a cell."

## Character Arc

- **Starting state:** A priest who has confessed privately, done penance, and believes that is enough.
- **Key turning points:** The cardinal's offer (ch6); refusing Rydel (ch7); deciding to confess from the pulpit (ch8); the confession itself and Zbyszek's answer; Wyszków (ch10).
- **Ending state:** Refused the bishopric, publicly confessed, exiled to a small parish, at peace with the not-choosing; still in love, still a priest.
