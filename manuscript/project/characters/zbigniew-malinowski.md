# Zbigniew Malinowski

## Appearance

Twenty-two, a collar too big for him, a suit too small.

## Personality & Traits

Angry, intelligent, and honest; the voice of the generation that has been told to remember nothing.

## Backstory

Works at the Nowotki works. Raised by an AK veteran who will not talk about his past. Serves Mass, plays table tennis, reads French theology.

## Motivations & Goals

To be told the truth by somebody.

## Voice & Speech Patterns

Blunt, young, liable to say the thing the room is thinking.

- "Nobody remembers. That is what this country runs on, Father."
- "I do not want a priest who has never been afraid. I want one who has."

## Character Arc

- **Starting state:** The angry boy in the pulpit's shadow.
- **Key turning points:** Speaking from the lectern on the fourth Sunday of Advent.
- **Ending state:** Called up, writing letters to Wyszków.
