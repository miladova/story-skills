# Brunon Kowalik

## Appearance

Forty-two; known in the novel through a photograph, a helmet, a pair of work gloves, and a letter.

## Personality & Traits

A careful man and a quiet complainer; not a hero, not a troublemaker.

## Backstory

Crane operator at the Zakłady Mechaniczne im. Nowotki. Wrote to the director in June 1958 asking for the crane cable to be replaced. Killed on 14 October 1958 when it snapped.

—

## Voice & Speech Patterns

Remembered through his letter: "crane number 4… I do not want to die."

## Character Arc

- **Starting state:** Dead before the story opens.
- **Key turning points:** His letter, which makes his death a thing with a document attached.
- **Ending state:** The dead man the sermon is about.
