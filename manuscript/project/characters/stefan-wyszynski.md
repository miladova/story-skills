# Stefan Wyszyński

## Appearance

Fifty-seven, tall, heavy-handed, with tired eyes and a gentle mouth; a fur-collared greatcoat and bad knees.

## Personality & Traits

Shrewd, unsentimental, funny in a dry way, and unbreakable. He negotiated the Church's position after 1956 and knows exactly what the state can and cannot be made to swallow.

## Backstory

Primate of Poland; interned 1953–56; released in the October thaw; negotiating the Church's narrow space ever since.

## Motivations & Goals

To keep the Church alive in Poland without a martyrdom he cannot afford.

## Voice & Speech Patterns

Anecdotal, authoritative, addresses the person and not the room.

- "They are not reading the books, Countess. The books are reading them."
- "I am not offering you a mitre. I am offering you a cross."
- "Then preach it well. A man's confession is a poor thing if it is badly said."

## Character Arc

- **Starting state:** The strategist who wants Janusz for the see.
- **Key turning points:** The refusal; ordering the transfer to Wyszków.
- **Ending state:** The same strategist, minus a candidate and plus a respect he will not say aloud.
