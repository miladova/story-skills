# Góra

## Appearance

Old and fat, a gold tooth, a gray coat and a fur hat.

## Personality & Traits

A survivor of every regime, shameless and cheerful about it.

## Backstory

The village headman under the Germans, who betrayed Rutka Kreiner and informed on Halina's mother. After 1944 he became a socialist and is now chairman of the collective farm.

## Motivations & Goals

To be on the winning side and to be seen paying his respects.

## Voice & Speech Patterns

Oily, self-congratulating.

- "Comrade Sowa. Welcome home."
- "The dead don't vote."
