# The frayed cable strand

## Description

A hand's length of steel cable, the outer wires snapped and splayed, given to Halina in a paper bag by Mieczysław, an old fitter who kept it "for the inspection."

## Function

Physical proof that the cable was worn when it was signed off as sound.

## History

Cut from crane number 4 after the accident.

## Current State

In Halina's drawer, then in a box of her belongings when she leaves Marszałkowska.
