# Brunon's letter

## Description

A sheet of cheap ruled paper, folded twice, written in a careful schoolroom hand: the crane is dangerous, the cable is old, he has written to the director in June and again in August, and he does not want to die.

## Function

It turns a workplace accident into a document: a man asked for the cable to be replaced and was ignored.

## History

Written in June 1958, kept by Wanda Kowalik, given to Halina in November.

## Current State

Locked in Halina's office drawer with the frayed cable strand and the truthful report she does not send.
