## Description

A baroque church with a tall tower and a facade the color of old cream, on Brzeska Street in Praga-Północ; the presbytery's roof carries a darker patch of tile where a bomb took it off in 1944. Inside: whitewashed walls, a side altar with a plaster Christ, a red sanctuary lamp, and a boiler that will not work.

## History

Survived the war; the parish held together under three rectors; eleven years of Janusz Wilga as curate and rector; a youth club with a table-tennis table and a library.

## Culture & Customs

Seven o'clock Mass, the widows in the front pews, the workers at the back, Pani Genowefa's tea after everything.

## Notable Features

The sacristy with its table and two chairs, the parish hall, the porch with the paraffin stove.

## Current State

December 1958: full on Sundays, watched on weekdays, and about to lose its rector.
