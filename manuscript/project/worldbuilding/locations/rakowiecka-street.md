## Description

A ministry building of gray corridors, green linoleum, a rubber mat at the door, a duty officer, and on the third floor an office with a window, a map of Warsaw, and a photograph of a girl.

## History

The security service's Warsaw seat; the place where files are kept and decisions about appointments are recommended.

## Culture & Customs

Tea in a glass, the folder tied with tape, the offer made in person.

## Notable Features

Rydel's office, with three folders, a brass ashtray, and a small bronze horse.

## Current State

December 1958: the room where the bishopric is refused.
