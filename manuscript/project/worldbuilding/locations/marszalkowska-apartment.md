## Description

A fourth-floor flat on Marszałkowska with a bare wall, a bookshelf hiding a novel, a wardrobe with a wooden box on top, and a window full of trams and the Palace of Culture.

## History

Halina's home for six years, paid for by the Party.

## Culture & Customs

None; it is a place she sleeps.

## Notable Features

The bare wall where the portrait of Marx used to hang; the drawer in the office, not the flat, where the truthful report is locked.

## Current State

December 1958: about to be vacated.
