## Description

A small town on the Bug, eighty kilometres north-east of Warsaw; burned in 1944 and rebuilt with new tin roofs and old statues rescued from the ruins; pines, a garrison, a market square, a hotel.

## History

Destroyed in 1944; a parish of four thousand souls and eleven villages, one of them Deskurów, whose chapel has not had a Mass since 1957.

## Culture & Customs

The town watches the priest; the priest is not invited anywhere twice after a rumour.

## Notable Features

The rebuilt church, the market square, the hotel where a woman from Warsaw takes a room once a year.

## Current State

1959: the place Janusz goes to be nobody.
