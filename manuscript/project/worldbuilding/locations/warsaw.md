## Description

A city of patched facades, empty lots, new apartment blocks with windows like dark eyes, trams that groan around corners, and the Palace of Culture needling the sky. Rebuilt, repopulated, and watched.

## History

Destroyed in 1944, rebuilt in the 1950s; the seat of the Central Committee, the security apparatus, and the Primate's palace; the site of the 1956 thaw and the slow narrowing that followed.

## Culture & Customs

Queues, permits, Party meetings, café vodka, village grandmothers in a city of five-story walkups.

## Notable Features

Marszałkowska, Rakowiecka Street, the Old Town, the Śląsko-Dąbrowski Bridge, Brzeska Street in Praga.

## Current State

December 1958: the thaw is ending, the Church is being squeezed, and everyone knows someone who has been questioned.
