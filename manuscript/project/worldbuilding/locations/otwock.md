## Description

A town of pines, peeling villas, and sanatoriums for people with lungs, south-east of Warsaw. Snow deep under the trees; a street of small wooden houses with carved verandas.

## History

A pre-war resort; a place where the security state parks people it does not want in the city.

## Culture & Customs

Quiet, careful, lace curtains.

## Notable Features

The house with the lace curtains and the wreath of dried leaves on the door; the railway line to Warsaw.

## Current State

December 1958: a kitchen where a widow is told the truth.
