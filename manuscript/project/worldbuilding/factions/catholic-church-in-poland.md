# Catholic Church in Poland

## Purpose

To be the Church in a country that has decided the Church must be managed. After 1956 it survives by negotiation, by the loyalty of the villages, and by the willingness of individual priests to be brave in public.

## Power Base

Four thousand parishes, the loyalty of the countryside, the moral authority of a Primate who was in prison for three years, and the fact that the Party's own children go to Mass.

## Members

Cardinal Wyszyński, Primate; the curia; Father Janusz Wilga of the Transfiguration; Canon Bochenek; the curates and catechists who keep the parishes open.

## Conflicts

Between those who would negotiate and those who would not; between the Primate's strategy of a narrow, patient survival and the state's determination to make every bishop a servant; between the Church's own memory of the war and the state's use of it.
