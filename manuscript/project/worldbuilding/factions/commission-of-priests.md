# Commission of Priests

## Purpose

A body of priests who have agreed to work with the state: to speak about peace, to support the government's church policy, and to give the Party a Catholic voice it can print.

## Power Base

The state's money and the state's microphone; the promise of a career, a parish, a mitre.

## Members

Priests who signed in 1950 and after; men who believe they are saving the Church, and men who believe they are saving themselves.

## Conflicts

The Commission is the test the state sets for Janusz: sign, and be a bishop; refuse, and be destroyed. Its existence divides the Church more effectively than any police action.
