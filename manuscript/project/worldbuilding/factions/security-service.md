# Security Service

## Purpose

To know, and to make sure the state knows first. In 1958 its work is less about the forest than about the office: informers among the clergy, notes taken in churches, files kept against the day they are useful.

## Power Base

The dossier, the dawn knock, the right to approve or refuse. Its officers make offers rather than threats, and the offers are real.

## Members

Rydel, a major with a soft face and a daughter; Major Zawistowski, who gives a young priest tea and a lecture on table tennis; the men in gray coats who stand in the fourth row with their hats on their knees.

## Conflicts

Rivalry with the Party apparatus over who owns the clergy file; a leadership that wants results without scandals; and the slow corrosion of officers who discover that the truth, when a man confesses it himself, is no longer a weapon.
