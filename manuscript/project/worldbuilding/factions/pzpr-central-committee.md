# PZPR Central Committee

## Purpose

The ruling party's central apparatus: policy, cadres, and the ideological management of a country that is two years out of a thaw and no longer sure how much rope to let out.

## Power Base

Nomination, employment, housing, the newspapers, the militia, and the files. The Party can end a career with a signature and a paragraph.

## Members

Halina Sowa, deputy head of the Propaganda and Agitation Department. Her superior, Kaczmarczyk, a gray man with a gray face who has survived four changes of line. The Control Commission, seven men behind a long table.

## Conflicts

Contains reformers who believe 1956 was a beginning and hardliners who believe it was a surrender; contains, also, people like Halina, whose private guilt has begun to make them unreliable.
